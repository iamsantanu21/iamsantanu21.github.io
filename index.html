I'm Santanu Mondal, from Kolkata, West Bengal, India. I recently completed my M.Tech in Network & Information Security at Pondicherry University, where my thesis designed a ZKP- and PUF-authenticated dual-offline CBDC protocol with AML/CFT compliance for resource-constrained IoT devices.

My research sits where blockchain, applied cryptography, and IoT security meet real-world constraints — from managing multiple identities of IoT devices on Hyperledger Fabric to zero-knowledge anonymous voting, and increasingly toward Edge AI and on-device inference. I've authored a peer-reviewed publication and several manuscripts currently under review.

I'm now seeking a PhD position, research role, or a computer-network / network-engineering role where I can keep building trustworthy, secure systems.
